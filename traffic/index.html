Daily activity

Every visitor who lands on the site (blue) plus every feature they use — point selections, searches, layer toggles (orange). August 24 — the day the rebrand and the fleet reorganisation shipped — is the single busiest day on record, at 133. August 25 is not a slump. Google Safe Browsing flagged the day-old districtry.com domain that morning, and Chrome shows its warning before the page loads, so blocked visits never reached the counter at all. The flag was a false positive and has since been cleared; the day is under-counted by an unknown amount and cannot be reconstructed. August 26 recovers to 65, and for the first time some of it lands on the Illinois SEO pages rather than the app: /il/police-district.html has drawn 14 visits and /il/school-board.html 4. August 27 is the strongest day since the rebrand at 93.

Every instance now has visitors, Iowa included. Six bars for five apps and the fleet landing page. Iowa is the newest and the note it carried yesterday is already out of date: through this window /ia was live and in the sitemap but not listed on the fleet landing page, which is how it drew a visit while offering four places to choose from — it joined the fleet manifest at 01:24 UTC on 28 August, just after this window closed, and the landing page now offers five. Most-toggled layers

Which district layers people actually reach for. Top 10 of those recorded.

The classic Chicago question wins: ward toggles nearly double the runner-up, with state and federal legislative layers close behind. One caveat on these ids: events share a single namespace across the fleet, so a layer that exists in more than one city (congress) would sum across them, while city-specific ids (ward = Illinois, council = New York, supervisor-district = San Francisco) are unambiguous. In this window the blending is still negligible — the four non-Illinois instances have 18 page visits between them against Illinois's 676 — so these counts are effectively all Illinois. That stops being true as the others grow.
