Daily activity

Every visitor who lands on the site (blue) plus every feature they use — point selections, searches, layer toggles (orange). August 24 — the day the rebrand and the fleet reorganisation shipped — is the single busiest day on record, at 133. August 25 is not a slump. Google Safe Browsing flagged the day-old districtry.com domain that morning, and Chrome shows its warning before the page loads, so blocked visits never reached the counter at all. The flag was a false positive and has since been cleared; the day is under-counted by an unknown amount and cannot be reconstructed. Since then it has settled into a band rather than a spike: 93, 66, 72 and 30 on the four days to August 30, against a pre-rebrand average of 39. The last of those is a Sunday, the quietest weekday here and the first day since the rebrand to fall below that average. The Illinois SEO pages keep compounding — /il/police-district.html is at 18 visits and /il/school-board.html at 14 — and August 27 remains the strongest day since the rebrand at 93.

By instance

Page visits split by path prefix — which app in the fleet people actually opened.

Six bars: five apps and the fleet landing page. Iowa joined the fleet manifest at 01:24 UTC on 28 August, so it is listed on the landing page now — for most of this window it was live and in the sitemap but unlisted, which is how it drew a visit from a page that did not offer it. Read the ranking as age, not quality: until August 24 the non-Illinois tags pointed at GoatCounter sites that did not exist and every one of their visits was dropped, unrecoverable. Illinois has seven weeks of history; the rest have days. The path widget lists its top ten, which covers 840 of the 841 page visits; the last two sit in paths below that cut.

Most-toggled layers

Which district layers people actually reach for. Top 10 of those recorded.

The classic Chicago question wins: ward toggles nearly double the runner-up, with state and federal legislative layers close behind. One caveat on these ids: events share a single namespace across the fleet, so a layer that exists in more than one city (congress) would sum across them, while city-specific ids (ward = Illinois, council = New York, supervisor-district = San Francisco) are unambiguous. In this window the blending is still negligible — the other five instances have 21 page visits between them against Illinois's 699 — so these counts are effectively all Illinois. That stops being true as the others grow.
